# Employee Onboarding Policy

## Probation Period
All new hires serve a probation period of ninety (90) days from their start
date, during which performance and fit are formally assessed.

## Orientation
New employees complete a mandatory two-day orientation program covering
company policies, systems access, and safety procedures.

## Equipment
Standard equipment (laptop, access badge, email account) is provisioned
before the employee's first day.
