# Procurement Policy

## Competitive Tender Threshold
Any purchase of five thousand dollars ($5,000) or more requires a
competitive tender process with at least three (3) quotes.

## Approval Authority
Purchases under $1,000 may be approved by a team lead. Purchases of $1,000
or more require department head approval.

## Preferred Vendors
Preferred vendors are reviewed annually and must be used for IT hardware
purchases where available.
