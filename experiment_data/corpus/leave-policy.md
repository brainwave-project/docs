# Annual Leave Policy

## Entitlement
Full-time employees accrue twenty (20) working days of paid annual leave per
calendar year. Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rata basis according
to their contracted hours.

## Carry-Over
Up to five (5) unused days may be carried into the following calendar year.
Days beyond this limit are forfeited on 31 December.

## Approval
Leave requests must be submitted at least five (5) working days in advance
through the HR portal and approved by the employee's direct manager.

## Public Holidays
Public holidays are not deducted from the annual leave balance.
