# Refund Policy

## Refund Window
Customers may request a full refund within thirty (30) days of the original
purchase date, provided the product is returned in its original condition.

## Process
Refund requests must include the original receipt or order confirmation
number. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within
seven (7) business days.

## Exceptions
Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable once delivered or
activated.
