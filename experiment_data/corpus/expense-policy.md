# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for pre-approved travel, accommodation, and
client-entertainment expenses incurred in the course of business.

## Submission Deadline
Expense claims must be submitted within fourteen (14) days of the expense
being incurred, accompanied by an itemized receipt.

## Approval Limit
Claims under $500 require manager approval only; claims of $500 or more
require additional approval from the finance department.
