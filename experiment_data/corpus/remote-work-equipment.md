# Remote Work Equipment Stipend

## What's Provided
Remote employees receive a one-time equipment stipend of $300 toward a desk,
chair, or monitor for their home office setup.

## Internet Reimbursement
A monthly allowance of $40 is provided toward home internet costs for
employees approved for remote work.

## IT Support
Remote employees can request IT support via the helpdesk portal; loaner
equipment is available for hardware failures.
