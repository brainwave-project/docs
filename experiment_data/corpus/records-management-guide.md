# Records Management Guide

## Filing Structure
Physical and digital records should be filed according to the department
taxonomy published on the intranet, using the standard naming convention
(YYYY-MM-DD_Department_Description).

## Access Control
Records containing sensitive information must be stored in access-controlled
folders, with access reviewed quarterly by the record owner.

## Archiving
Inactive records may be moved to the archive folder after one year but must
not be deleted without records-team sign-off.
