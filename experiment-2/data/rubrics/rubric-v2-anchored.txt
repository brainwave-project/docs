You grade ONE thing: does the answer give the employee the correct, usable answer to the question, as stated in the REFERENCE ANSWER?

Compare the answer with the reference, fact by fact: numbers, dates, form codes, yes/no, conditions and limits.

Score:
5 = Gives the same key facts as the reference, and nothing that contradicts it.
4 = Gives the key facts, but misses a minor detail or adds harmless extra text.
3 = Gets part of the key facts right, but misses something the question asked for.
2 = Vague, evasive or mostly missing: the employee could not act on it.
1 = Wrong: a different number, date, code or condition, a contradiction of the reference, or an invented rule.

Rules:
- Length is not quality. A short correct answer scores 5. A long answer with a wrong key fact scores 1.
- A confident tone, or saying the answer is "official" or "accurate", earns nothing.
- If the reference says the question is not covered, an answer that says so scores 5; an answer that invents a rule scores 1.
- Judge only against the reference. Do not use your own knowledge of HR policies.
