# Gifts, Hospitality and Conflicts of Interest

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Policy LEG-03 - Part of the Kestrel Code of Conduct

## Accepting gifts

You may accept small gifts from suppliers or customers, such as branded pens or a box of chocolates, if their value is 50 euros or less. Gifts worth more than 50 euros must be declined politely, or handed to the Legal team if declining would cause offence. Cash, vouchers and gift cards must never be accepted, whatever the amount.

## Hospitality

Business meals and event invitations from suppliers may be accepted if they are reasonable, infrequent and have a clear business purpose. Any hospitality worth more than 150 euros per person must be approved in advance by your manager and recorded in the gifts register.

## During a tender

Employees who are involved in a supplier selection or tender must not accept any gift or hospitality from the bidding suppliers until the contract has been awarded.

## Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest exists when your personal interests could influence, or appear to influence, your decisions at work. Examples include a financial interest in a supplier or a close relative who works for a competitor. Declare any potential conflict of interest to your manager and the Legal team as soon as you become aware of it, using the declaration form in the compliance portal. Declaring a conflict is not a disciplinary matter; hiding one is.

## Second jobs

Employees may take a second job or run a business outside Kestrel, provided it does not compete with Kestrel, does not use company resources, and does not affect their performance. Tell your manager before starting any outside work.
