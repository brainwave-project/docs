# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Policy HR-11 - Effective 1 March 2026

## 1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to employees whose role can be done away from a Kestrel site. Roles in the warehouse, the test laboratory and the reception desk are site-based and are not eligible for regular remote work.

## 2. The hybrid pattern

Eligible employees work from the office on at least two anchor days per week, Tuesday and Thursday, and may work remotely on the other days. Teams can agree a different anchor day with their department head, but every team must spend at least two days a week together on site.

## 3. Working from another country

Working remotely from outside your country of employment is allowed for up to 20 working days in a calendar year. This limit exists for tax and immigration reasons. Any request to work abroad must be approved in advance by the People Team, even for a single day, because some countries require a work permit for any work at all.

## 4. Equipment and home office

Kestrel provides a laptop, a monitor, a keyboard and a headset for every eligible employee. In addition, each employee may claim a one-off home office allowance of 300 euros for a desk chair or desk, through the expense system within the first six months of starting hybrid work. Equipment remains company property and must be returned when employment ends.

## 5. Internet and utility costs

The company does not reimburse home internet, electricity or heating costs, because hybrid work is optional for eligible employees. Employees whose contracts designate them as home-based are the exception: they receive a fixed monthly payment of 40 euros to cover these costs.

## 6. Security when working remotely

Remote work must follow the IT Security Policy. In particular, you must connect to company systems only through the company VPN, you must not use public computers, and you must lock your screen whenever you leave your device, including at home. Confidential papers must not be printed at home.

## 7. Health and safety

Employees are responsible for setting up a safe workspace at home. A self-assessment checklist is available in the HR portal and must be completed within the first month of hybrid work. If the checklist identifies a problem, the Facilities team will advise on adjustments.

## 8. Availability and core hours

Remote employees must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 local time on working days, which are the company's core hours. Outside core hours, employees can organise their time flexibly, as long as their contracted hours are worked and meetings are attended.

## 9. Changing or ending the arrangement

A manager may ask an employee to work on site more often for a limited period, for example during a product launch, with at least one week's notice. If the hybrid arrangement is not working, the manager and employee should first try to agree changes. The company may end an individual arrangement with one month's written notice.

## 10. Requesting a fully remote contract

Employees who want to become permanently home-based must submit a flexible working request through the HR portal. The company will respond within two months and must give business reasons if the request is refused.
