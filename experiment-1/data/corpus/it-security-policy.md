# IT Security Policy

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Policy IT-01 - Effective 1 January 2026

## Passwords and sign-in

Use a password of at least 14 characters for your company account. Multi-factor authentication is required for all company systems, including email and the HR portal. Never share your password or approve a sign-in prompt that you did not start.

## Company devices

Only company-managed devices may be used to access customer data. Personal phones may be used for email and chat only after the Kestrel device profile is installed. Do not install software on a company laptop unless it is listed in the software catalogue.

## Lost or stolen devices

If a company laptop, phone or security key is lost or stolen, report it to the IT Service Desk within one hour of noticing, by calling the service desk or using the chat. The device will be locked and wiped remotely. You should also report the theft to the police and send the report number to the service desk.

## Phishing

If you receive a suspicious email, do not click links or open attachments. Use the Report Phishing button in the email client. Reporting a phishing email that you clicked by mistake is always the right thing to do, and no disciplinary action will be taken for honest mistakes that are reported promptly.

## Data classification

Company information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data, such as salary information and customer contracts, must be stored only in approved systems and must never be sent to a personal email address.

## USB storage

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops by default. If you need to transfer files to a customer on a USB drive, request an encrypted drive from the IT Service Desk.
