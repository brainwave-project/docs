# Performance Review Process

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Guide HR-09 - Effective 1 January 2026

## The review cycle

Kestrel runs one annual performance review in December and a shorter mid-year check-in in June. Between reviews, managers and employees should meet for a one-to-one conversation at least once a month.

## Setting objectives

Each employee agrees three to five objectives with their manager at the start of the year. Objectives should be specific and measurable and should be recorded in the performance system by the end of January.

## Ratings

At the annual review, the manager gives an overall rating on a four-point scale: Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Partly meets expectations, and Does not meet expectations. Ratings are checked in a calibration meeting with other managers in the same department before they are shared with employees.

## Self-assessment

Before the annual review, every employee completes a self-assessment in the performance system. The self-assessment must be submitted at least one week before the review meeting.

## Pay and bonus

Salary increases and bonuses are decided after the annual review and take effect on 1 March. An employee rated Does not meet expectations is not eligible for a bonus for that year.

## Disagreeing with a review

If you disagree with your rating, first discuss it with your manager. If the disagreement is not resolved, you can ask for a review by your manager's manager within 14 days of receiving the rating.

## Performance improvement plans

When performance is below expectations, the manager and employee agree a performance improvement plan of up to three months, with clear targets and regular check-ins.
