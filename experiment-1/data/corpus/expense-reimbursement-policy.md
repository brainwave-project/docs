# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Policy FIN-02 - Version 4 - Effective 1 February 2026 - Replaces version 3 (2021)

## Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims in the expense system within 60 days of the date the cost was incurred. Claims submitted later than 60 days will not be reimbursed unless the finance director approves an exception. Every claim needs an itemised receipt; a card payment slip alone is not accepted.

## Approval and payment

Claims are approved by the line manager. Claims above 1,000 euros also need approval from the budget holder. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary, provided they are approved by the 20th of the month.

## Meals

When you travel for business, meals are reimbursed up to a daily limit of 60 euros. Meals with clients are reimbursed at actual cost, provided the names of the attendees and the business purpose are recorded on the claim. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except as part of a client meal and within reasonable limits.

## Mileage

Use of a private car for business journeys is reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometre. Travel between home and your normal place of work is not a business journey and cannot be claimed.

## Items that are never reimbursed

The following are never reimbursed: fines and penalties, including parking fines; personal entertainment; upgrades to business class unless approved under the Business Travel Policy; and costs already paid by a client.

## Lost receipts

If a receipt is lost, complete the missing receipt declaration form FIN-17 and attach it to the claim. A missing receipt declaration can be used at most three times per year.
