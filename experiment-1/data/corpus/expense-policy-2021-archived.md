# Expense Reimbursement Policy (ARCHIVED)

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Policy FIN-02 - Version 3 - Effective 1 March 2021

SUPERSEDED on 1 February 2026 by version 4. This copy is kept for audit purposes only. Do not use it for new claims.

## Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims within 90 days of the date the cost was incurred. Every claim needs a receipt.

## Meals

Business travel meals are reimbursed up to a daily limit of 45 euros. Client meals are reimbursed at actual cost with the attendees recorded.

## Mileage

Use of a private car for business journeys is reimbursed at 0.25 euros per kilometre.

## Lost receipts

If a receipt is lost, write a short note to the finance team explaining the cost.
