# Business Travel Policy

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Policy FIN-05 - Effective 1 February 2026

## 1. Principles

Travel only when a meeting cannot reasonably be held online. When travel is needed, choose the option that gives the best value for the company, taking cost, time and your wellbeing into account.

## 2. Booking

All flights, trains and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal, TravelDesk. Bookings made elsewhere will only be reimbursed if they are at least 15% cheaper than the lowest TravelDesk fare available at the time, and a screenshot of the TravelDesk fare is attached to the claim. Book at least 14 days before departure whenever possible, because late bookings are usually more expensive.

## 3. Class of travel

Economy class is the standard for all flights. Premium economy may be booked for flights with a scheduled duration of more than 6 hours. Business class requires approval from a member of the executive team before booking. For rail travel, standard class is used for journeys under 3 hours, and first class may be used for longer journeys.

## 4. Hotels

Choose hotels within the nightly limits published on TravelDesk. The limit is 150 euros per night in most cities and 220 euros per night in high-cost cities such as London, Zurich and New York. Hotel stays above the limit need approval from your line manager before booking. Room service, minibar and in-room films are personal costs.

## 5. Per diem

For each full day spent travelling on business, you may claim a per diem of 45 euros to cover incidental costs such as local transport, tips and small purchases. The per diem does not cover meals, which are claimed under the Expense Reimbursement Policy. Half days of travel receive half the per diem.

## 6. Travel insurance

All business trips are covered by the company's travel insurance. The policy includes medical treatment abroad, lost luggage and trip cancellation. Before travelling, download the insurance card from the HR portal and keep it with you. Personal trips added to a business trip are not covered for the personal days.

## 7. Travel safety

Before booking travel to a country with a high risk rating, you must get approval from the Security Officer and complete a short safety briefing. The current risk ratings are listed on TravelDesk. While abroad, keep your manager informed of any significant change to your plans.

## 8. Combining business and personal travel

You may add personal days to a business trip, provided the extra days do not increase the cost to the company. Any extra cost, such as a more expensive return flight or additional hotel nights, must be paid by you.

## 9. Loyalty points

You may keep airline and hotel loyalty points earned on business travel, but you must not choose a more expensive supplier in order to collect them.

## 10. Travel for candidates and new hires

Travel for job candidates is booked by the People Team, not by the hiring manager. New hires who relocate may be eligible for a relocation package, which is agreed in their offer letter.
