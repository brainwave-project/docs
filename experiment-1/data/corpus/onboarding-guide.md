# New Starter Onboarding Guide

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Guide HR-02 - 2026 edition

## Before your first day

You will receive an email from the People Team about one week before you start. It contains your start time, the address of your site, and a link to complete your personal details and bank information in the HR portal.

## Your first day

On your first day, report to reception at 9:00. Bring a photo identity document, because we need to check your right to work before you can start. Your manager or an onboarding buddy will meet you at reception.

## Equipment

Your laptop will be ready on your first day. The IT Service Desk will help you set up multi-factor authentication during your first morning. If you need special equipment, such as an ergonomic keyboard, tell your manager before you start so that it can be ordered in time.

## Your buddy

Every new starter is given an onboarding buddy from a different team. Your buddy is not your manager; they are a friendly contact who can answer everyday questions during your first three months.

## Mandatory training

All new starters must complete four online courses within 30 days of starting: information security, data protection, health and safety, and the Code of Conduct. The courses are assigned automatically in the learning platform.

## Probation

The probation period lasts six months. Your manager will hold review meetings at the end of your first, third and sixth months. During probation, the notice period on either side is two weeks.
