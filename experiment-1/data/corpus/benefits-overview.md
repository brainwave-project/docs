# Employee Benefits Overview

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Guide HR-12 - 2026 benefits year

## 1. Pension

Kestrel operates a workplace pension scheme. Employees are enrolled automatically after one month of employment. The company matches employee contributions up to 6% of basic salary. For example, if you contribute 4%, the company also contributes 4%; if you contribute 8%, the company contributes 6%. You can change your contribution rate once a month in the benefits portal.

## 2. Private health insurance

All employees are covered by private medical insurance from their first day. The cover includes specialist consultations, diagnostic tests and hospital treatment. Employees can add a partner and children to the policy at a discounted rate, paid through salary deduction. Dental and optical treatment are not included in the medical insurance; see section 3.

## 3. Dental and optical cash plan

The cash plan refunds part of the cost of routine dental check-ups, eye tests and glasses. Each employee can claim back up to 400 euros per benefits year. Claims are made directly with the cash plan provider, not through the expense system.

## 4. Life assurance

Employees are covered by life assurance of four times basic salary from their first day. You can nominate who should receive the payment through the benefits portal. Keep your nomination up to date after life events such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## 5. Learning and development budget

Each employee has a personal learning budget of 1,000 euros per calendar year for courses, conferences, books and certifications that are relevant to their role or career at Kestrel. The budget does not carry over to the next year. Spending more than 500 euros on a single course needs manager approval. If you leave Kestrel within 12 months of completing a course that cost more than 2,000 euros, you may be asked to repay part of the cost.

## 6. Employee assistance programme

The employee assistance programme offers free and confidential support 24 hours a day, including counselling, legal information and financial guidance. Up to six counselling sessions per issue are covered. The service is also available to members of your household.

## 7. Cycle to work scheme

Employees can lease a bicycle and safety equipment through salary sacrifice, which reduces tax and social security on the cost. The scheme is open once a year, during the benefits window in November.

## 8. Wellbeing allowance

Each employee receives a wellbeing allowance of 25 euros per month, which can be spent on a gym membership, sports classes or a mental wellbeing app. The allowance is paid with the monthly salary and is taxable.

## 9. Changing your benefits

Most benefits can be changed only during the annual benefits window in November, or within 30 days of a life event such as marriage, divorce or the birth or adoption of a child. Changes are made in the benefits portal.

## 10. Leaving Kestrel

Private health insurance, life assurance and the employee assistance programme end on your last day of employment. Pension contributions stop with your final salary, but the money in your pension remains yours.
