# Parental Leave Policy

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Policy HR-07 - Effective 1 January 2026

## Birth parent leave

The birth parent is entitled to up to 52 weeks of leave. Kestrel pays the first 18 weeks at full salary, followed by statutory pay for the remaining paid period. At least 15 weeks' notice before the expected week of childbirth must be given to the People Team.

## Partner leave

A partner of the birth parent, including an adoptive or same-sex partner, is entitled to 4 weeks of partner leave at full pay. Partner leave can be taken in one block or in two separate blocks of 2 weeks, and must be used within the first 6 months after the birth or placement.

## Adoption leave

The primary adopter receives the same entitlement as a birth parent, starting from the date the child is placed. The second adopter receives partner leave.

## Keeping-in-touch days

During parental leave you may work up to 10 keeping-in-touch days without ending your leave. These days are paid at your normal daily rate and are always voluntary.

## Returning to work

You can return to the same role after up to 26 weeks of leave. After a longer absence, you return to the same role or, if that is not reasonably practicable, to a similar role on the same terms. You may request flexible working on your return; see the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.
