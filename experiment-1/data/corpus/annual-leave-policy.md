# Annual Leave Policy

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Policy HR-04 - Effective 1 January 2026 - Owner: People Team

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how much paid holiday employees receive, how to request it, and what happens to unused days at the end of the year. It applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees. Contractors are covered by the Contractor Handbook instead.

## 2. Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted hours. After five years of continuous service, the entitlement rises to 28 days. Employees who join or leave part-way through the year receive a proportional share of the annual entitlement, rounded up to the nearest half day.

## 3. Public holidays

The company observes the public holidays of the country in which the employee is contracted. If a public holiday falls on a day an employee does not normally work, no replacement day is given. Employees who are required to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day off, which must be taken within three months.

## 4. Requesting leave

All leave must be requested through the HR portal before it is taken. Requests of five days or fewer need at least two weeks' notice. Requests longer than five consecutive working days need at least four weeks' notice and approval from both the line manager and the department head. Managers should respond to a request within five working days. A request that has not been answered within that time may be escalated to the People Team.

Leave requests are normally approved on a first-come, first-served basis. During the annual stock-take in the last two weeks of June, the warehouse and finance teams may only take leave in exceptional circumstances.

## 5. Carrying over unused leave

Employees may carry over a maximum of five unused days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March; any carried-over days not used by that date are lost. Days above the five-day limit are forfeited on 31 December unless the employee was prevented from taking leave by sickness or parental leave, in which case the People Team will agree a later date.

## 6. Buying and selling leave

Once per year, during the benefits window in November, employees may buy up to five additional days of leave through salary sacrifice. Selling leave back to the company is not permitted, except for untaken statutory leave when employment ends.

## 7. Leave during the notice period

When an employee resigns, the manager may ask them to take outstanding leave during their notice period. Untaken leave that remains on the last day of employment is paid in the final salary. Leave taken in excess of the pro-rata entitlement is deducted from the final salary.

## 8. Sickness during annual leave

An employee who falls ill during annual leave can reclassify those days as sick leave, provided they notify their manager on the first day of illness and supply a medical certificate for the period. The reclassified days are returned to their annual leave balance.

## 9. Frequently asked questions

**Can I take leave in half days?** Yes. Half days can be booked in the HR portal and count as 0.5 of a day.

**Do I need to be reachable on holiday?** No. Employees are not expected to read email or answer calls while on annual leave. Managers should arrange cover before the leave starts.

**What if my manager refuses a request?** The manager must give a reason in the portal. If you disagree, you can raise the matter with the People Team.

## 10. Review

The People Team reviews this policy every year. Questions about this policy should be sent to people@kestrel.example.
