# Sick Leave Policy

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Policy HR-05 - Effective 1 January 2026

## Reporting an absence

If you are too ill to work, tell your line manager by phone or message before 9:30 on the first day of absence. An email to a shared inbox is not enough. Keep your manager updated at least every three days.

## Self-certification and fit notes

For absences of up to seven calendar days, you can self-certify by completing the absence form in the HR portal when you return. For absences longer than seven calendar days, you must provide a fit note from a doctor.

## Company sick pay

Employees receive full pay for up to 20 working days of sickness in any rolling 12-month period, and half pay for a further 20 working days. Company sick pay starts after the probation period ends; during probation, only statutory sick pay applies.

## Return to work

After any absence longer than five working days, your manager will hold a short return-to-work meeting. The purpose is to check that you are well enough to work and to agree any temporary adjustments, such as reduced hours or a phased return.

## Medical appointments

Routine medical and dental appointments should be booked outside working hours where possible. If that is not possible, up to two hours per appointment can be taken as paid time, with your manager's agreement.
