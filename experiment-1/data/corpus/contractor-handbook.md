# Contractor Handbook

For independent contractors and agency workers engaged by Kestrel Components Ltd.

## Status

Contractors are not employees of Kestrel. The policies in the employee handbook, including annual leave, sick pay, parental leave and employee benefits, do not apply to contractors unless their contract says otherwise.

## Time off

Contractors agree their availability directly with the project manager. Contractors should give at least one week's notice of planned absences so that project plans can be adjusted.

## Expenses

Contractors invoice travel costs only if their statement of work allows it. Travel must still be booked through TravelDesk.

## Equipment and access

Contractors receive a company laptop only when their work requires access to internal systems. All access ends automatically on the last day of the statement of work.

## Invoicing

Invoices are paid 30 days after they are received by the accounts payable team, provided they quote a valid purchase order number.
