# Speak-Up and Whistleblowing Procedure

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Procedure LEG-07 - Effective 1 January 2026

## What to report

You should report any concern about wrongdoing at Kestrel, such as fraud, bribery, danger to health and safety, environmental damage, or a breach of law. You do not need proof; a reasonable belief is enough.

## How to report

You can raise a concern with your manager, with the Legal team, or through the independent Speak-Up hotline, which is run by an external provider and is available 24 hours a day in several languages. Reports to the hotline can be made anonymously, by phone or through its website.

## What happens next

Every report is acknowledged within 7 days. The Legal team decides who should investigate, making sure that no one involved in the concern is part of the investigation. The person who raised the concern receives feedback on the outcome within three months, where this is possible without breaching confidentiality.

## Protection from retaliation

Kestrel does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded. Retaliation, such as dismissal, demotion or harassment of a whistleblower, is a disciplinary offence.

## False reports

Knowingly making a false report is a disciplinary offence. Reports that are made honestly but turn out to be mistaken are never treated as false reports.
