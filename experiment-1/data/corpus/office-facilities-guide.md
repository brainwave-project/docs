# Office and Facilities Guide

Kestrel Components Ltd. - Head office, Harbour Street site

## Opening hours

The head office is open from 7:00 to 20:00 on working days. Access outside these hours needs approval from the Facilities team.

## Desks and meeting rooms

Most desks are shared. Book a desk or a meeting room in the workplace app up to four weeks in advance. The office is busiest on team anchor days, so book early.

## Parking and bicycles

There are 40 parking spaces, allocated first to employees with accessibility needs and to visitors. A secure bicycle store with showers is available on the ground floor.

## Kitchen and food

Free coffee, tea and fruit are available on every floor. A subsidised canteen serves lunch between 12:00 and 14:00.

## Visitors

Register visitors in the workplace app before they arrive. Visitors must sign in at reception and wear a visitor badge at all times.

## Lost property

Lost property is kept at reception for one month.
